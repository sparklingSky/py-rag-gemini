**Looker** is a product that helps you explore, share, and visualize your company's data so that you can make better business decisions. This page will help you start learning Looker with our documentation.

## Personalizing Looker

[Personalizing Looker](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/user-menu) --- Choose personalization options for your Looker user profile.

## Landing pages

These introductory pages help you learn Looker's various skills:

- [Latest features](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/looker-releases) --- Read about the new features in Looker's most recent release.
- [Keyboard shortcuts in Looker](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/keyboard-shortcuts) --- See Looker's keyboard shortcuts.
- [Find and organize content](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/find-and-organize-content) --- Learn how to find the Looks and dashboards that others in your company have already made, and learn how to organize that content.
- [Send and share content](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/send-and-share-content) --- Learn how to share the content you've found or created, along with how to send content and data to other services.
- [Retrieve and chart data](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/retrieve-and-chart-data) --- Learn how to pull data out of Looker and turn it into tables and graphs.
- [Create and edit dashboards and Looks](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/dashboards-and-looks-intro) --- Learn how to create and edit dashboards and Looks in Looker.
- [Get ready for development](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/data-modeling) --- Learn about setting up and working in Looker's developer environment.
- [Write LookML](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/write-lookml-intro) --- Learn how to write LookML, which is Looker's language for describing your SQL data and the options you want for your users.
- [Use the API and embedding](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/use-embedding-and-api) --- Learn how to embed Looker data in various ways and how to use our API.
- [Set up and administer Looker](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/set-up-and-administer-looker) --- Learn how to set up Looker for the first time, connect Looker to your database, and use Looker's system administration functions.
- [Enabling the mobile application](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-enablement) --- Learn how to enable, install, and use the Looker mobile application.
- [Looker (Google Cloud core) documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/looker-core) --- Learn how to create, administer, and observe and troubleshoot a Looker (Google Cloud core) instance.
- [Improve your skills in Looker and Data Studio](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/build-skills-with-courses) --- View an overview of Looker and Data Studio courses and labs, organized by skill or learning path.
- [Looker (Google Cloud core) quickstart overview](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/looker-core-qs-overview) --- View the quickstarts that are available to help you get started with Looker, from connecting to your data warehouse to creating and sharing dashboards with rich visualizations.
